activation of Italy’s NEC The Minister made these remarks during the Ecofin discussion on the activation of the National Escape Clause for energy and defence  November 09, 2026 Copy link Share on: Brussels, 9 November 2026 – “ We welcome the approval of the Recommendation with satisfaction: the national escape clause enables us to strengthen defence capabilities and energy security, while remaining fully consistent with the recommended net expenditure path and safeguarding the sustainability of public finances ”. This was stated by Giancarlo Giorgetti, Minister of Economy and Finance , during the Ecofin discussion on the activation of the National Escape Clause (NEC) for energy and defence for Italy. “ The 2027 Budget Law, of which the clause is an integral part ,” said Giorgetti, “ provides for the use of the flexibility allowed under the NEC for defence-related expenditure, up to a maximum of 0.3% of GDP in both 2027 and 2028. In addition, a further margin, also equal to 0.3% of GDP for each of the two years, is envisaged for measures aimed at strengthening energy resilience. In particular, we strongly and proudly claim the inclusion of energy within the NEC. It was an Italian initiative that will be beneficial to all European countries that choose to make use of it .” “ We have held extensive discussions with the Commission ,” he continued, “ regarding the energy security measures that we intend to implement by making use of the flexibility provided for under the national escape clause. These measures primarily concern the development of renewable energy generation and storage, energy-efficiency improvements in schools, hospitals and other public buildings , as well as sustainable mobility , including local public transport and railway infrastructure .” “ Implementation will be a crucial aspect: the Commission will assess, ex post, the actual expenditure incurred for each individual measure; I am confident that the initiatives undertaken will comply with the eligibility criteria. To this end, Italy will provide semi-annual updates ,” the Minister concluded, “ on the measures for which the application of flexibility is requested .” Regarding tobacco taxation , Italy supports the compromise proposal put forward by the Irish Presidency of the Council of the European Union on the revision of EU rules. Giorgetti announced this during the public session of the Ecofin meeting.“ In order to achieve an appropriate balance among various objectives, while also accommodating different concerns and taking economic realities into account, the amendments introduced by the Presidency concerning the definitions of tobacco products and, most recently, the treatment of raw tobacco are acceptable to us ,” the Minister stated, “ but they should not be subject to further revision .” “ With regard to the distributed document on nicotine pouches, and considering the sensitivity of the issue for some Member States, including Italy, we share the Presidency’s proposal ,” he continued. “ Overall, we believe that the Presidency’s compromise text strikes the right balance among the different requirements and, therefore, we are able to support it .”   Photogallery Ecofin | Bruxelles 9 November 2026 | Flickr This embedded image gallery via Flickr uses third-party cookies.
