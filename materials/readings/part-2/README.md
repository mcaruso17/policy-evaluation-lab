# Part II readings: macroeconomic policy and its evaluation

Downloaded 7 November 2026. All files are free to read: NBER working papers,
EU and ECB documents, a Bruegel policy brief, the Nobel committee's background
note. Books at the end are not included (not freely available).

If time is short, read in this order (about 10 hours):
1. Nakamura and Steinsson (2018), the bridge from Part I to Part II
2. Ramey (2019)
3. Darvas, Welslau and Zettelmeyer (2024)
4. ECB strategy overview (2025)
5. Juhasz, Lane and Rodrik (2023)
6. Draghi report, Part A

## What is in the repository

This repository is public, so only the EU and ECB documents (free reuse with
attribution) are stored here. The NBER working papers, the Bruegel brief and
the Nobel background note are listed above with their full references and can
be downloaded from NBER, Bruegel and nobelprize.org; a complete offline copy
was delivered separately as `part2-readings.zip`.
